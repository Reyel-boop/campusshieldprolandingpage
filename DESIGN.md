## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-readiness visual identity crafted specifically for institutional decision-makers—superintendents, campus security directors, and school boards. It projects military-grade reliability, compliance precision, and operational clarity while remaining reassuring and human for educators and parents.

### Visual Style
The design style merges **Corporate / Modern Enterprise** with **Tactile Command Center Precision**:
- **Structured Authority:** Architectural density, razor-sharp alignment, and high-legibility typographic hierarchies eliminate ambiguity during both routine daily operations and critical crisis moments.
- **Operational Clarity:** High-contrast utility accents guide immediate scanning, using tactical badges, telemetry-inspired data metrics, and verified compliance markers to reinforce institutional integrity.
- **Controlled Urgency:** Emergency protocols and crisis responses are highlighted with calculated, high-visibility contrast rather than chaotic alarmism, maintaining calm control and commanding respect.

## Colors

The color system relies on high-contrast, functionally segregated color assignments to distinguish governance, operational security, and urgent emergency response.

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Deep Institutional Navy):** Represents ultimate authority, structural stability, and governance. Applied across primary headlines, solid CTA containers, enterprise navigation, and dominant grounding panels.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - High-Trust Deep Emerald Teal):** Denotes active perimeter defense, verified campus entry, automated check-ins, and standard operational clearance. Used for primary conversion triggers, positive metrics, verified credentials, and active system pings.
- **Tertiary (`#EF4444` - Alert Crimson):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate threat response, lockdown triggers, automated crisis notifications, and emergency system states. It must never be diluted by use in general non-critical marketing graphics.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral Palette):** Bridges dense information architectures with functional subtext, supporting borders (`#E2E8F0`), muted surface backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`), and clean canvas white (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure uncompromised data density and scannability.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an intentional dichotomy:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** anchors marketing claims and structural section headers. Its geometric balance provides a modern, forward-thinking institutional presence without sacrificing corporate gravity.
- **Inter** handles narrative flow, telemetry outputs, feature comparisons, and transactional lead capture. Its neutral grotesque cadence guarantees rapid legibility across dense data tables, incident logs, and technical compliance matrices.

### Scaling & Readability Directives
- `display-hero` collapses smoothly to `display-hero-mobile` below 768px to eliminate orphaned headline phrasing in top-funnel value propositions.
- Numerical statistics, incident response time counters (e.g., `< 3.2s`), and badge micro-labels must always use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure strict alignment in comparison cards and live dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored within a maximum container width of `1280px` (`max-w-7xl`).

### Responsive Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `margin: 1rem` (16px) side padding and `gutter-sm: 1rem` (16px) vertical flow. Lead capture forms switch from side-by-side inputs to vertical stacking. Sticky bottom CTA appears on critical conversion scroll points.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column active grid with `margin-md: 2rem` (32px) margins and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Feature comparison matrices convert into horizontal swipe containers or segmented tabs.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12-column layout with `margin-lg: 4rem` (64px) to `margin-xl: 6rem` (96px) section margins. Gutter spaces expand to `gutter-lg: 2rem` (32px), creating distinct visual lanes between sales copy and interactive demo calculators.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Inter-section spacing follows a strict rhythm of `space-3xl` (72px) on desktop and `space-2xl` (48px) on mobile.
- Form inputs, field groups, and card internals rely uniformly on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) for rapid tactile target identification.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids excessive blur stacks and floating skeuomorphic artifacts, choosing instead **Tactical Low-Contrast Outlines paired with Targeted Directional Lighting**. Depth signifies functional hierarchy and security layer elevation:

- **Flat/Ground Tier (0):** Pure `#FFFFFF` or structured `#F8FAFC`. Used for core canvas surfaces, informational comparison columns, and baseline data lists. Delimited with sharp 1px `#E2E8F0` borders.
- **Elevated Interactive Tier (1):** Core functional cards, lead forms, and interactive incident calculators. Features a 1px solid border (`#CBD5E1`) combined with a crisp, low-intensity ambient falloff: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Command & Floating Tier (2):** Sticky verification bars, live security alert previews, and floating lead acquisition prompts. Features a dual shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with an upper 1px micro-highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- **Critical Alert State (Tier 3):** Lockdown and threat response demonstration components employ an inset perimeter ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #EF4444, 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`), conveying high operational readiness without visual noise.

## Shapes

The system implements a **Soft / High-Precision (`1`)** shape language:
- Standard components (buttons, input fields, badges) use `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) corner radiuses.
- Larger cards, telemetry modals, and visual preview containers use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Major container boundaries or modal shells never exceed `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).

This controlled angularity reflects structured hardware integration, government-grade compliance certificates, and security hardware aesthetics, rejecting the toy-like impression of large circular radiuses.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Conversion CTA:** Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) or High-Trust Teal (`#0D9488`) solid fill with pure white typography, 4px border radius, 48px height on desktop (`44px` minimum on mobile for touch compliance), and bold `label-lg` text. Hover states shift darkness by 8% with a slight upward 1px translateY.
- **Urgent Lockdown / Threat Simulator CTA:** Crimson fill (`#EF4444`) with high-contrast white text, flanked by warning shield icons. Used strictly in live product simulation demos.
- **Secondary / Demo Outline Button:** 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, transitioning to `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Compliance & Security Seals:** Background `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.25)`, text `#0D9488`. Uppercase `label-mono-sm` tracking with a centered 6px pulsating status dot.
- **Threat Level Badges:** Crimson variant with `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)` background, `#EF4444` border, and text for simulated emergency lockdown indicators.

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Lead Capture Form Card:** Elevated surface using a crisp 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded-lg`, interior padding of `space-xl` (32px), containing trust proof-points immediately below the submit trigger (e.g., "FERPA / DRRM Compliant • 256-bit Encryption").
- **Comparison Metric Tile:** Baseline surface `#FFFFFF` anchored with large numeric stat headers (`headline-lg` in `#0D9488` or `#0F172A`), followed by concise `body-sm` description labels.

### Input Fields & Lead Capture Controls
- **Height & Spacing:** Standardized 44px field height with 12px horizontal inset padding.
- **Border States:** Default `#CBD5E1`; focus transitions to a 1.5px `#0D9488` ring with zero outline offset.
- **Labels & Helper Text:** `label-md` seated above field with 6px spacing; error notifications display immediately below in `#EF4444` `body-sm`.

### Lists & Social Proof Grids
- **Checkmark Lists:** Institutional benefit points feature filled `#0D9488` rounded-badge checkmark icons paired with standard `body-md` text.
- **Client & District Trust Banners:** Grayscale brand marks rendered at 60% opacity against an off-white `#F8FAFC` band, transitioning to 100% brand tone on hover to establish verified third-party validation.
